Business tax reminders

Items to review before year-end and before filing season.

Records

Close the books monthly

Reconcile bank and credit card accounts, review open receivables and payables, and document owner draws, distributions, loans, and reimbursements before tax season.

Payroll

Verify payroll and contractor data

Confirm employee addresses, withholding, benefit deductions, contractor W-9s, and 1099 totals before W-2 and 1099 filing season.

Planning

Review tax exposure early

Review year-to-date income, estimated payments, depreciation, retirement contributions, state filings, and available deductions before decisions become rushed.

Entity compliance dates

DMV-area entity maintenance reminders.

Entity compliance rules vary by entity type and jurisdiction. Confirm your exact due dates with the state or with your CPA before relying on a general calendar.

JurisdictionGeneral reminderSource
